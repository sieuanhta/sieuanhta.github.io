01

About

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at UET–VNU interested in mathematical and computational approaches to decision-making under uncertainty. My current research spans mechanism design, statistical learning, scientific machine learning, and quantitative finance.

I am a Research Assistant in the Human–Machine Interaction Laboratory at UET–VNU, supervised by Dr. Duyen Thi Ngo, and a researcher at Beetrade. My undergraduate dissertation studies the price of learning quality in finite-inventory information procurement.

02

Research

Finite-Inventory Information Procurement

I study data procurement when inventory is finite, suppliers behave strategically, and quality is initially unknown. The work develops truthful mechanisms, randomized auditing schemes, and guarantees on the cost of learning quality.

Manuscript under review

Online Coalition Formation

I study how to maintain stable coalitions as agents arrive sequentially, drawing on hedonic games, online algorithms, and multi-agent systems.

Manuscript under review

Derivative-Faithful Neural Networks

I investigate population-informed neural surrogates that recover first- and second-order differential information from sparse, value-only observations, with emphasis on differential identifiability and derivative accuracy.

Manuscript in preparation

Quantitative Trading & Market Microstructure

At Beetrade, I develop and evaluate medium-frequency strategies using historical market data, covering signal construction, backtesting, portfolio construction, and execution.
